Features

Prepaid expenses.

Now 1C:Drive supports the prepaid expenses functionality. This helps companies account for advance payments for future services, such as annual software subscriptions or insurance policies.

Product tolerance.

Since this release, 1C:Drive allows to register shipment of products in the quantity greater than the sales order quantity.

Improvements

Exchange rate profile.

To improve exchange rates storage and management, we introduced exchange rate profiles.
